# The Founding 100 story

## What

The Founding 100 page + checkout + 100-cap are live. The remaining question is
**how the first 100 are found** — a campaign decision, not code. Tied to
`distribution-quietlaunch` (ROADMAP §Now item 6).

## Done-conditions (testable once drafted)

- [ ] A written answer to "where do the first 100 come from?" — channels, the
      ask, the patron framing, the timeline.
- [ ] Mapped to the GTM phases (Phase 1 friends/alpha → Phase 2 community).
- [ ] A clear success metric (spots claimed per phase; cost per acquisition if
      paid).

## Why

The cap is a real business-health guardrail (lifetime plans are risky past a
point). Selling 100 lifetime memberships is its own campaign — it deserves a
decision, not a hope.

## Open questions

- Founding 100 as a loss leader for case studies/testimonials vs as a revenue
  line? (Lean: the former — their value is the proof they generate, more than
  the $13,900.)
- Cap the rate (e.g. ≤10/week) or sell until gone? (Lean: until gone — scarcity
  is honest, throttling invented scarcity would violate PRODUCT.md.)
