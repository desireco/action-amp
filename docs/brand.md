## 0. The one-line idea

**ActionAmp is the attention engine.** The "superhuman" feeling does not come
from luxury language — it comes from **decisiveness delivered calmly**. An app
that opens to one task, the next thing that matters, and means it. That
*is* an unfair advantage, stated honestly. No badges, no streaks, no hustle.

This is the Superhuman (the email app) model, not the Rolex model: premium
earned through **craft + decisiveness + restraint**, never through executive
signaling. It keeps every `PRODUCT.md` ban intact while delivering the
"we're not like the others" presentation the product deserves.

## 1. Positioning

> **ActionAmp is the attention engine for people who freeze at "what do I do next?"**
>
> Every other todo app opens to a list of everything you have to do — which is
> exactly the problem. ActionAmp opens to **one task**, the next thing that
> matters, and hides the rest. You capture everything; you only ever see what to
> do *now*. The decision, not the capture, is where attention is lost.

### Value proposition

> **For** people overwhelmed by a hundred things to do **who** freeze at the
> decision of what to do next, **ActionAmp** is the attention engine **that**
> opens to one task — the next thing that matters — and hides the rest,
> **unlike** every todo app that opens to a list, **because** the decision, not
> the capture, is where attention is lost.

## 2. Tagline hierarchy

| Layer | Line | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Category** | Attention Engine | Hero eyebrow, meta description, one-liners |
| **Hero tagline** | One task. The next one that matters. | Landing hero — the single biggest line |
| **Value-prop sub-line** | From intention to action. | Hero sub-head, supporting placement |
| **Internal north star** *(never public)* | Superhuman attention. | Positioning benchmark only — trademark/confusion risk; do not ship in copy |

**Why this composition:** the hero tagline is the *proof* of the category.
"Attention Engine" is the claim; "One task. The next one that matters." *does*
what an attention engine does — gives you one thing. Show, don't tell. The
tagline demonstrates the product truth rather than asserting it.

## 3. Voice

### Where ActionAmp sits (the four spectrums)

| Spectrum | Position | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Formal ↔ Casual | Center, leaning formal | Direct prose, no slang — but never corporate |
| Simple ↔ Complex | Simple, conceptually deep | Plain words carrying big ideas. Compressed, not dumbed down |
| Serious ↔ Playful | Serious, warm | We take attention seriously. Warmth comes from honesty, not jokes |
| Reserved ↔ Expressive | Reserved with conviction | Few words, each loaded. Confidence shows in restraint |

### The four traits

| Trait | We do | We don't |
|---|---|---|
| **Decisive** | State the thesis plainly. Pick the one. | Hedge, qualify, or list every option |
| **Calm** | Whitespace is the point. Silence carries weight. | Manufacture urgency, hype, or energy |
| **Honest** | Admit what's not built. State real scarcity only. | Fake momentum, invent scarcity, or overclaim |
| **Crafted** | Every detail considered. Fast, precise, quiet. | Feel generic, cobbled, or "another todo app" |

**Premium lives in Decisive + Crafted.** That is the whole strategy — the load-
bearing line of this document. A calm app that makes one decision, instantly,
beautifully, and means it. Luxury language is not needed and is off-register.

## 4. Lexicon

### Adopt (these words do work)

> attention · amplify · intention · action · the decision · one · next ·
> what matters · surface · hide the rest · quiet · considered

### Retire from marketing

| Word | Why |
|---|---|
| ~~productivity~~ | Commodity — lands you in the $3/mo bucket instantly |
| ~~tasks~~ | The implementation, not the promise. Fine in-product; never as hero copy |
| ~~timer / pomodoro~~ | The feature, not the value |
| ~~streak / badge / guilt~~ | Banned entirely (`PRODUCT.md` §Fair to users) |
| ~~elite / luxury / exclusive~~ | Luxury signaling — off-register for calm-premium |
| ~~focus~~ *(as the hero noun)* | Every app claims "focus." Use sparingly as a verb/state; **attention** is the hero noun |

### The load-bearing distinction: attention vs. focus

**Attention** is the noun — the asset, the muse, what's scarce. **Focus** is what
you do with it. Attention leads; focus supports. Never lead copy with "focus" —
it surrenders the differentiation to the commodity category.

## 5. Messaging

### Supporting pillars

| Pillar | Line | What it proves |
|---|---|---|
| **Differentiation** | The list is the problem. | Every other app opens to a list. We open to a decision. |
| **Method** | Capture the firehose, surface the next drop. | The thesis: overwhelm is at the decision, not capture. |
| **Register proof** | Calm by design. | We compete on being worth it, not on anxiety. No streaks, no guilt. |

### Elevator pitches

**10-second:**
ActionAmp opens to one task — the next thing that matters — and hides the rest.
It's not a list. It's a decision.

**30-second:**
Every todo app opens to a list of everything you have to do, which is the
problem. ActionAmp opens to one task — the next thing that matters — and hides
the rest. Capture everything, see only what to do now. An attention engine, not
a task manager.

**60-second:**
The matcher decides what's next — not you — and tells you why. GTD + PARA depth
for power users, invisible to beginners. No streaks, no badges, no guilt. Calm
by design; premium because it's considered, not because it shouts.

### Message by audience

ActionAmp is **built for anyone overwhelmed** (the `PRODUCT.md` users
definition) — we do not market to a named segment. The calm-premium register
*reaches* founders, executives, and creators through craft, not by labeling
them. If a surface ever needs a segment angle, lean on the outcome (finish what
matters) and the register (calm, decisive), never on identity labels.

## 6. Do / Don't (the guardrails)

### Do

- Lead with the one task. Show the product truth.
- Let whitespace and restraint signal confidence.
- Name attention as the scarce asset.
- State real scarcity honestly (the Founding 100 cap).

### Don't

- Open any line with "a productivity app that…" — you're in the commodity
  bucket instantly.
- Borrow "superhuman" in public copy (internal benchmark only).
- Use executive-luxury language (elite, leverage, "$100k decisions") — it
  breaks the calm register.
- Add streaks, badges, challenges, or identity pricing tiers — banned.

## 9. Provenance

This kit was refined 2026-07-09 from a positioning conversation that explored
executive-luxury and "Superhuman for X" directions. ~40% of that conversation
conflicted with ActionAmp's locked identity (`PRODUCT.md` bans + ADHD-muse
positioning + calm register). What survived and composes here: (a) invent a
category rather than compete in "productivity," (b) own the word "Amp" =
amplify, (c) the daily ritual framing (one question → one task), (d) premium
positioning earned through craft/decisiveness rather than luxury language. What
was discarded: identity pricing tiers, badges/challenges, "$100k proposal"
copy, and "Superhuman for X" as public tagline. See the decision log §8.
