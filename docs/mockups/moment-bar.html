Gap B — decisions locked by this mockup

Placement
Above the card, inside the card's top padding. The bar is metadata about the moment, not about the task — so it sits above the task title, not inside the context line (which would crowd the most-read line) and not below the why-line (which would read as a verdict on the picker).
Format
Two segmented controls (time: 15m / 30m / 1h / 2h+; energy: low / med / high). Not chips (chips imply multi-select); not a form (a form implies commitment). Segmented controls are the calmest way to present 4 + 3 mutually-exclusive options. Active segment uses teal — system/state, never amber (amber is reserved for Important per PRODUCT.md).
Collapse
Default-collapsed to one quiet line: "30m · low energy — change". Click anywhere on the line, or press T / G, to expand. Collapses back on selection or blur. The collapsed line is the whole point — the bar is barely there until you touch it. Calm over features.
Inference
Time-of-day default, stated explicitly: "30m · low energy — inferred from time of day". Morning → high, post-lunch → low, evening → medium. The first time it appears, the inference is named; once the user overrides, the override sticks for the session and the "inferred" tag disappears. Never hidden, always overridable.
Mismatch (State 3)
When the user's moment disagrees with the top task, the bar stays calm and the wn-why line states it plainly ("...but it's XL and you have 15 min. Sure?"). No red, no nag, no blocking — priority is never demoted by fit (per the spec's core invariant). The matcher explains; the user still clicks Do this.
What is not here
No badge counts, no "you've avoided this," no streak of any kind (PRODUCT.md bans them). No third selector (location, mood, etc.) — two is the cap; a third would crowd the wedge surface. No persistence beyond the session — the moment is now, not a preference.
