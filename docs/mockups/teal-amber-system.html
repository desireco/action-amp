Two-accent system · teal primary, amber secondary

Teal carries the system. Amber carries the warmth.

Two accents work when they have non-competing roles. Teal is everything the app does for you — completion, selection, focus, the primary action. Amber appears only when something needs your human attention — Important priority, the "why this?" nudge, an XL-size warning. Amber is never the default; that's what keeps it from becoming stressful.

Teal — system / state
The app talking to you. The default accent.
  • Completion circle fill
  • Selection & active states
  • Primary CTA buttons
  • Focus-mode glow & progress
  • Links & "open" affordances
Amber — human emphasis
Where your attention is genuinely needed. Rare.
  • "Important" priority chip
  • The "why this?" focus nudge
  • XL-size break-down warning
  • Momentum / streak (Phase 2)
  • Today-cap warning ("1 slot left")
