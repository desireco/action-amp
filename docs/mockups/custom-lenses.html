2 · Settings → Lenses tab (Pro)

New tab at the end of the settings sub-nav. Lists every lens with its identity color, purpose, counts, and kind. Rename / edit-purpose / recolor apply to all lenses; delete is CUSTOM only (the two seeded lenses can't be deleted — they're the stable handles).
