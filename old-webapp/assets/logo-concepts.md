# Logo Concepts

## Direction 1: A with arrow around

This direction treats the logo as motion first. The circular arrow gives the mark a sense of repeated action, iteration, and momentum, while the center `A` keeps the app name readable at small sizes.

Best use: product mark, app icon, favicon candidate.

## Direction 2: A with battery

This direction treats the logo as energy first. The battery frame suggests charge, capacity, and power, with the `A` acting like the active charge indicator.

Best use: brand mark when the "amp" part of Action Amp should feel more literal.

## Recommendation

Start from the arrow concept if the product is mainly about doing, triggering, or accelerating actions. Start from the battery concept if the product is mainly about energy, capacity, usage, or boosting performance.
