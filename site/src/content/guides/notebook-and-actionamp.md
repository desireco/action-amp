Paper is excellent at the present moment: meeting notes, sketches, a rough plan, a page of frustration. Digital systems are better at remembering commitments after the page is closed. Neither needs to impersonate the other.

Use this division:

- **Notebook:** thinking, notes, daily scratch work, and context.
- **ActionAmp:** tasks, projects, dates, next actions, and review.

## The handoff

During a meeting or planning session, write freely. Put one simple mark beside anything that becomes a commitment. At the end of the day, capture only marked commitments into ActionAmp’s Inbox. Do not transcribe the whole page.

At weekly triage, scan recent pages for remaining marks. Capture them, then trust the notebook to remain a record rather than a second task list.

One action should exist in one place. The notebook can explain it; ActionAmp should remember it. That boundary is what makes a hybrid system feel lighter instead of doubled.
