“Redo website” is not a task. It is a project: multiple actions, decisions, and a finish line. Putting it on a task list is a small lie: it looks actionable while hiding the work required to start.

Use this test:

- A **task** can be completed in one sitting with one visible action.
- A **project** needs more than one task and has a defined outcome.
- A **goal** is the larger result the project supports.

Turn “redo website” into a project. Then give it one next action: “collect three examples of product pages I like.” The task list becomes a list of doors you can open, not a list of rooms you must somehow enter all at once.
