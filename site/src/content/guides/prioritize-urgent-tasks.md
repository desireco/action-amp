Urgent is often a feeling wearing the clothes of a fact. Treat it as real only when somebody is waiting, something breaks, or delay has a cost.

## Sort by consequence

- **Deadline:** What has a real date or person waiting on it?
- **Dependency:** What unblocks another person, decision, or project?
- **Cost of delay:** What becomes harder, more expensive, or more stressful tomorrow?

Tasks with no answer belong below tasks with one. A loud task is not automatically a consequential task. This is not a productivity trick; it is permission to stop treating every request as an emergency.

## Do not use one giant priority list

Choose at most three live priorities for the day: one consequence task, one progress task, and one small maintenance task. Everything else stays available, but not competing for the same attention.

If a new urgent item arrives, replace one of the three explicitly. Hidden additions are how a calm plan becomes a panic list.
