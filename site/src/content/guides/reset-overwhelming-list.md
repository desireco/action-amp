Do not read an old list as a contract. It is a pile of messages from a past version of you, many of which are no longer addressed to today.

## Four passes, not a full rewrite

1. **Now:** Keep only items with a current consequence or a clear next action.
2. **Later:** Move real ideas and future commitments out of your daily field of view.
3. **Reference:** Save information that is useful but not actionable.
4. **Let go:** Delete stale obligations, duplicate reminders, and tasks you no longer intend to do.

Give each item a fast answer. If it needs research to decide, move it to later with a date to reconsider. Do not turn cleanup into a new research project. A backlog is not a museum; it is allowed to lose things.

Finish by choosing one task from Now. A reset works only when it returns you to action.
