You do not need every GTD list before you begin. Most systems fail because they ask for a tidy model of your life before they have helped you do anything. Start with five things.

1. **Inbox:** One fast place for every incoming thought.
2. **Triage:** A recurring moment to decide what inbox items are.
3. **One goal:** The outcome you are trying to move.
4. **One project:** The current chunk of work toward that outcome.
5. **One next task:** A concrete action you can start today.

Capture freely. Triage weekly. Let structure appear when real work demands it. A system should earn its complexity.

The system is working when you trust that a thought can leave your head, land somewhere safe, and return only when you are ready to decide it. More categories do not create that trust. Regular decisions do.
