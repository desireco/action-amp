Plans fail when they assume every day has the same capacity. Instead of asking what the ideal version of you would finish, ask what today can support. A plan that only works on excellent days is not a plan; it is a fantasy schedule.

Keep three kinds of next actions:

- **Deep:** requires clear attention and uninterrupted time.
- **Steady:** ordinary work you can complete in a normal window.
- **Light:** small administrative or maintenance work for low-energy periods.

Choose from the right lane, not from one undifferentiated list. A light day is not a failed deep-work day. It is a different context.

Protect one deep action when conditions allow. On other days, make an honest smaller promise and keep it.
